# Recheck documentation

Recheck keeps Confluence honest about what is out of date. A page counts as confirmed when it is edited or when someone marks it reviewed; after the space's review interval it is stale until someone confirms it again.

## The state under the title

Every page shows its state under the title: **Up to date**, **Reviewed 2026-11-08**, **Review due in 12 days**, or **Stale: review overdue by 7 months**. Click it to see when the page was last edited and reviewed, and who owns it.

## Review a page

Open **Apps > Review** in the page's more-actions menu (the three dots). You can:

- **Mark as reviewed**, with an optional note about what you checked. The clock restarts from today.
- **Snooze 30 days** when the page needs work but not right now.
- Give the page **its own review interval** (30, 60, 90, 180 or 365 days), or go back to the space default.

## The space dashboard

Open **Page reviews** under the space's apps in the sidebar. It shows how many pages are stale, due soon and up to date, and a table of pages with the most overdue first. Mark a page reviewed or snooze it from the table. Switches show only stale and due-soon pages, or only the pages you own. The dashboard checks the 500 least recently edited pages of the space.

## Space settings

Space settings > Apps > **Page reviews**: the review interval (default 180 days), how early a page shows as due soon (default 14 days), and **weekly reminders**. With reminders on, once a week Recheck posts a comment on each newly stale page that mentions its owner, at most 20 pages per space per week, and never twice for the same stale period.
